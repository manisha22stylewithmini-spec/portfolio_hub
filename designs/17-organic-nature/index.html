Our way

From seed
to skin

Every bottle follows the same slow path through the seasons. No shortcuts, no greenhouses.

1

Sow

Heirloom seeds planted by hand in spring, in soil we've fed for thirty years.

2

Gather

Flowers picked at dawn, when their oils are richest and the bees are still asleep.

3

Infuse

Steeped for six full weeks in cold-pressed oils, turned by hand each morning.

4

Bottle

Poured into amber glass and labelled with the date it was harvested.
