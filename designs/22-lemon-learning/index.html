About me

The way I learn is different.

I picked learning because of design thinking. They fit together: both start with a person, a messy problem and a lot of curiosity.

So I treat every new subject like a design brief. I don’t ask “what do I have to remember?” I ask “what is this for, and who needs it?”

  1. 1
    Empathise → NoticeSit with the topic. What confuses me? What do others get stuck on?
  2. 2
    Define → QuestionTurn the fog into one clear question worth answering.
  3. 3
    Ideate → SketchDraw it, map it, explain it badly first. Many rough ideas beat one perfect one.
  4. 4
    Prototype → TryBuild a tiny version and learn from what breaks.
  5. 5
    Test → ReflectShare it, listen, keep what is sweet and rinse what is sour.
A little update

What this site is.

This is my notebook, made public: a short story of how I learn, a few honest notes on what I’m working on, and every project I’ve built with Claude.

The lemon is the guide. Every sticker on it is a thing I’ve tried, a question I asked, or something I got wrong and kept anyway. As you scroll it turns, so you see a different side of me.
