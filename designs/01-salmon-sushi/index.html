Barely Waved, Beautifully Served

We start before sunrise, with the day's catch still cold from the water. Each fillet is trimmed by hand, rested, and sliced against the grain so it melts the moment it meets the rice.

Nothing is hidden behind heavy sauces. A touch of citrus, a leaf of dill, a grain of flaked salt — just enough to let the salmon speak for itself.

It is a slow kind of food, made for people who like to taste the quiet details.
