TheQuietSeason

— Editor's letter

An ode to restraint. Cashmere the colour of late light, silk that moves like water, and tailoring cut so precisely it hardly needs to speak.

Look01
Silk charmeuse gownIvory

Less noise.
More meaning.

This season, the atelier returned to its oldest idea: that true luxury is felt rather than announced. Every piece in the collection began on the cutting table as a single line, drawn and redrawn until nothing could be taken away.

We sourced wool from a single family mill in Biella, silk woven slowly on century-old looms in Como, and leather tanned with bark and patience. The palette is drawn from stone, smoke and the deep red of a cellar wine.

What remains is a wardrobe made to be lived in for decades — clothes that grow softer, quieter and more yours with every wear.
