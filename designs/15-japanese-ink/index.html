一期一会 ― 一生に一度の出会い
Ichigo ichie · one time, one meeting

Quiet is the rarest luxury left in the world.

For four generations our family has kept the same promise: tatami that smell of fresh rushes, a cedar bath under the stars and a cup of tea made slowly, as if it were the only one. Leave your watch at the door. Here, time is measured in steam and birdsong.

Rooms

Eleven rooms,
eleven silences

Each room faces its own garden and is named after the painting that hangs in its alcove.

春
Haru · Spring

Under the cherry trees

Breakfast on the engawa as petals fall into the koi pond. Hanami picnics prepared by our kitchen.

夏
Natsu · Summer

Fireflies and cold soba

Evening walks along the stream, yukata cool against the skin, lanterns floating on the river.

秋
Aki · Autumn

Maple fire

The hills turn crimson. Moon-viewing on the roof with chestnut sweets and warm sake.

冬
Fuyu · Winter

Snow on the bath

The cedar onsen steams under falling snow. Hot pot by the irori hearth until midnight.

Chanoyu

The way of tea

一
Purify

Wash the hands, quiet the mind

At the stone basin in the garden, cold mountain water marks the threshold between the world and the tea room.

二
Prepare

Water, fire and bamboo

Our tea master warms the bowl and measures the matcha with a bamboo scoop carved by her grandfather.

三
Whisk

A green, living foam

Fifteen seconds of whisking. The sound is part of the ceremony — like rain on a paper screen.

四
Receive

Turn the bowl, and drink

Turn the bowl twice so its most beautiful side faces your host. Drink in three slow sips. Bow.
